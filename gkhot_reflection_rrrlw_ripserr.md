# Reflection: rrrlw_ripserr

ripserr is pretty irregular: almost nothing in 2018-2019, a big spike in 2020, then long quiet periods. It has 6 gaps of 3+ months, and the longest is 21 months (2023-01 to 2024-09).

The commit messages didn't explain the gap, so I checked GitHub. The last commit before the gap was part of PR #34, which was opened in December 2022 but not merged until October 2024. A couple of issues from 2022 (#30, #31) also stayed open. So I think the maintainers just didn't have time to review things.

The gap ended when Cory Brunson merged #34 and updated the docs. He was already contributing before the gap, so the same person brought it back. The repo later moved to the tdaverse org and got really active in 2025 (80 commits between April and September), so it's Active as of 2025-09-30.

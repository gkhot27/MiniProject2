# Reflection: dakep_pense-rpkg

pense is almost entirely one developer, davidkep. It was busy in 2016-2017, almost dead in 2018-2019, and active again in 2020-2022, so I called it U-shaped. It has 6 gaps of 3+ months, and the longest is 7 months (2016-09 to 2017-03).

Before the gap, he released two hotfix versions (0.5.5 and 0.5.6) and fixed a few bugs. After the gap, in April 2017, the same person came back with bug fixes and new options for the lambda grid.

This one was harder to interpret because nothing in the commits or issues explains the break. Since it's a one-person research package, my best guess is that he was busy with other work and came back when he needed new features. It's Active as of 2025-09-30.

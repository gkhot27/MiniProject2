# Reflection: igsio_openigtlinkio

This project peaked in 2016-2017 and has only had a few commits a year since 2019, so I called it declining. It has the most gaps of my projects (11 gaps of 3+ months). The longest is 11 months, 2019-11 to 2020-09.

Before the gap, Kyle Sunderland and Adam Rankin were adding features and fixing bugs, and nothing suggested the project was stopping. I think it was just stable and didn't need changes. The restart was clearer: in October 2020 the commits are all about fixing compilation with VTK 9 (#82, #85), and issue #83 reports a build error. So a new VTK release breaking the build is what brought people back.

The recovery was a mix of old contributors (Adam Rankin, Kyle Sunderland) and new ones (Simon Drouin, Ole Vegard Solberg). It's Active as of 2025-09-30, and recent commits are still mostly build fixes.

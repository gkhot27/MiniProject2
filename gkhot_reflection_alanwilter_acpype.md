# Reflection: alanwilter_acpype

ACPYPE usually gets 50-100 commits a year, but there's a big spike in 2021 (about 300) and it drops off after that. I called it irregular. It has 6 gaps, all exactly 3 months long, so the "longest" one is just the first: 2019-04 to 2019-06.

None of these gaps really look like abandonment. Before the first one, Alan Silva (the author) was fixing file parsing bugs and lkagami added Anaconda packaging. After it, Alan came back in July 2019, updated the docs and citations, and made a new release. To me it just looks like a solo maintainer taking a short break.

The same person brought it back every time. It's Active as of 2025-09-30. Most of the recent commits are Dependabot updates that Alan merged.

# Reflection: declaredesign_estimatr

estimatr had most of its activity in 2017-2018 and dropped every year after that, so I called it declining. It has 5 gaps of 3+ months, and the longest is 11 months (2022-08 to 2023-06).

The gap starts right after Graeme Blair submitted a new version to CRAN ("submitted to cran"). After that, a mature package doesn't really need commits. People still opened issues during the gap, but most got few or no replies, and the "New version 1.0.0" PR (#394) stayed open the whole time.

The gap ended in July 2023 when Graeme Blair merged #394 and updated the docs, so the same people brought it back, but activity stayed low after that. The last commit before the cutoff was February 2025, so it's Inactive as of 2025-09-30.
